Lindon's Thunderstorm Season: Preparing Your Roof for Impact

Lindon, nestled along the Wasatch Front, experiences its fair share of dramatic weather, especially as we head into the warmer months. Thunderstorms, with their accompanying high winds, heavy rain, and sometimes hail, are a common occurrence. These storms can quickly turn a minor roof issue into a major problem, leading to leaks, structural damage, and costly repairs. Proactive preparation is key to safeguarding your home.

Before the storm clouds gather, it's wise for Lindon homeowners to assess their roof's current condition. Look for loose or missing shingles, clogged gutters, and any signs of wear that could be exacerbated by severe weather. Addressing these vulnerabilities now can prevent significant damage later, ensuring your home remains a safe haven against the elements.

Common Types of Storm Damage in Lindon

When thunderstorms sweep through Lindon, they can inflict various types of damage on your roof. High winds are notorious for lifting and tearing off shingles, leaving your underlayment exposed to the elements. Heavy rainfall can exploit even the smallest gaps, leading to water intrusion and interior damage. Hail, while less frequent, can cause impact damage, bruising shingles and compromising their protective granules, which can accelerate their deterioration.

Beyond the obvious, storm damage can also manifest as debris accumulation, such as fallen branches or leaves, which can clog gutters and cause water to back up under your shingles. It's crucial to understand that not all storm damage is immediately visible from the ground. Some issues, like minor shingle bruising or granule loss, might only be apparent upon a professional inspection, but can lead to significant problems if left unaddressed.
