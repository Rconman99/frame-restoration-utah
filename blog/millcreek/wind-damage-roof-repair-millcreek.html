Gusty winds common in the Millcreek corridor can lift shingles, loosen fasteners, and compromise flashing around chimneys and vents. Common Signs of Wind-Related Roof Damage

Look for curled or missing shingles, dented gutters, and debris trapped in valleys. Check ridge caps for displacement and flashing for separation at roof-plane intersections. Interior signs include water stains near attic rafters or ceiling corners. These issues often appear subtly after wind events but can lead to significant leaks if ignored. Frame’s inspectors document all findings with photos for insurance clarity.

How to Safely Assess Roof Damage After a Windstorm

Never climb onto a roof during or immediately after high winds. Use binoculars from the ground or a drone with a camera for initial visual checks.
