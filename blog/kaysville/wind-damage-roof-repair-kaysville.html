Common Wind Damage Signs Kaysville Residents Should Monitor

Kaysville homeowners should regularly inspect for specific wind damage indicators, particularly in exposed areas like north-facing slopes and gable ends. Look for peeling or missing shingles, dented or separated flashing around chimneys and vents, and granule accumulation in gutters — these are early warnings of wind uplift. Ridge caps that appear loose or misaligned often signal high-pressure zones during storms. Additionally, check for water stains on ceilings or musty odors in attics, which may indicate hidden leaks. Frame Restoration Utah advises monthly visual checks during storm season and recommends binocular inspections for multi-story homes. Gutter maintenance can prevent secondary water damage.
