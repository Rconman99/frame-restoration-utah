Identifying Summer Thunderstorm and Wind Damage in Payson

High winds can easily lift asphalt shingle tabs, breaking the critical thermal sealant bond that binds shingle courses together. Once this factory seal is compromised, wind drives air, rain, and debris underneath the protective barrier, creasing fragile fiberglass mats and stripping protective mineral granules. Homeowners suspecting storm damage should schedule a professional storm damage roof inspection to catch hidden vulnerabilities before rain enters the home interior.

Wind-driven rain can rapidly enter minute openings around vulnerable roof penetrations, including plumbing pipe boots, attic exhaust vents, metal valleys, and chimney flashings. In established Payson neighborhoods near South Main Street, Gladstone, and West Mountain, older roofing systems are particularly susceptible to wind uplift and thermal seal failure during sudden summer downpours.
