Identifying Storm Damage: What to Look For After a Thunderstorm

After a thunderstorm, especially one with strong winds or hail, it's important to carefully inspect your property for signs of damage. On your roof, look for missing, cracked, or curled shingles. Dents on gutters, downspouts, or even your car can indicate hail impact. Granule loss from asphalt shingles, often visible in downspouts, is another tell-tale sign of hail damage. Don't forget to check for debris accumulation, which can trap moisture and accelerate deterioration.

Inside your home, be vigilant for water stains on ceilings or walls, which are clear indicators of a roof leak. Even small stains can signify a larger problem that needs immediate attention.
