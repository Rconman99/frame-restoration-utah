What We Do

Roofing Work We Handle in Salt Lake City

Each project starts with the roof in front of us. We look at the deck, flashing, drainage, ventilation, materials, and damage pattern before recommending a scope.

Roof Replacement

When repairs no longer make financial or technical sense, we plan the tear-off from the deck up. The scope covers damaged sheathing, flashing details, underlayment, ventilation, and a roofing material suited to the property and its exposure.

Roof Repair

Leaks, missing shingles, failed flashing, wind lift, and isolated storm damage do not automatically require a full replacement. We trace the failure, photograph the affected areas, and explain whether a focused repair can safely extend the roof’s service life.

Roof Installation

For new homes, additions, and commercial construction, we coordinate the roof as a complete water-management system. That includes the deck, underlayment, penetrations, valleys, wall transitions, ventilation, and final material installation.

Hiring a Roofing Contractor in Salt Lake City — Quick Facts

Roof repairs in Salt Lake City typically run $400–$2,500 and full replacements $9,000–$28,000+, depending on size, pitch, and materials. How We Work

How We Plan Salt Lake City Roofing Work

A useful roofing proposal should make the condition, options, and tradeoffs easy to understand. Our process is built around evidence from the property and a scope the owner can review before work begins.

A Scope Based on the Roof

We inspect visible roofing, flashings, penetrations, drainage paths, and accessible attic conditions. The recommendation follows what those details show, whether that points to a repair, a phased plan, or replacement.

Clear Comparisons Before You Choose

We separate required work from optional upgrades and explain assumptions about decking, ventilation, flashing, cleanup, and warranty terms. That gives property owners a fair way to compare scopes instead of comparing one bottom-line number.

Built for Salt Lake Weather

Roof systems here move through snow loads, freeze-thaw cycles, canyon winds, hail, and long periods of UV exposure. Material choice matters, but so do fastening, intake and exhaust ventilation, ice protection, and the transitions most likely to leak.

Connected Exterior & Restoration Work

When a roofing issue also affects gutters, interior finishes, or storm-restoration work, Frame can keep the connected scope with one team. We also handle commercial roofing, gutters and gutter guards, water and fire restoration, and solar installation.

Local Knowledge

Roofing Salt Lake City, Neighborhood by Neighborhood

Salt Lake City isn’t one roofing market — each neighborhood has its own architecture, permit rules, and weather exposure. Here’s how our crews approach the areas we work in most.

The Avenues

Historic-district homes often need Planning Commission review before a material change. We match period-appropriate profiles and handle the design-review paperwork.

Sugar House

1920s–40s bungalows run low-pitch with shallow attics. We spec Class A architectural shingles (required city-wide) and add ventilation where the original build fell short.

Capitol Hill & Federal Heights

Tile and standing-seam metal are common, but some overlays restrict materials — we verify the overlay before quoting metal so nothing gets rejected at permit.

East Bench

Steep lots and Wasatch-compressed wind call for premium architectural or metal systems and upgraded fastening. We build for the exposure, not the average.

Downtown & West Side

Hard surfaces, lower shade, and summer heat can increase roof-surface stress. We compare lighter shingle colors, intake/exhaust ventilation, and drainage upgrades on west-side reroofs.
