At more than 5,000 feet, the Heber Valley gets long, cold winters and heavy snow. That means pipes freeze in exterior walls, crawlspaces, and garages; ice dams form along eaves and push meltwater under shingles; and second homes and cabins can sit with a leak for days before anyone notices. When the snow melts, runoff raises groundwater and finds its way into basements.

Because Frame Restoration Utah is based in Heber City and works as both a roofer and a restoration contractor, we can fix the roof or flashing that let the water in and restore the damage inside.

Why do Wasatch Back homes get so much winter water damage? +
Long, cold winters freeze pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, and vacant second homes, and heavy snow loads lead to ice dams that push meltwater under shingles and into ceilings. Spring runoff then raises groundwater around foundations.
