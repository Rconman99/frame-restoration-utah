After a Midvale Hail or Wind Event

  • Photograph hail, gutters, and downspouts from the ground. Dented soft metals and granule washout help document what the roof may have taken.
  • Do not climb on a wet or bruised roof. Storm-damaged shingles can be slick, and foot traffic can make damage worse.
  • Call 435-292-8802 if water is entering the home. Emergency tarping and leak stabilization come first.
  • Schedule a free inspection within days. Hail bruising, lifted tabs, and flashing movement are easier to read before sun and weather obscure the damage pattern.
