Bountiful roof inspection notes

Bountiful roofs often need careful east-bench documentation. We separate downslope wind wear, snow-shed patterns, chimney flashing, valley drainage, and shaded north-slope aging from sudden hail or wind damage so the homeowner can see what condition belongs to which cause.

Homes near Orchard Drive, the foothill streets, and older Davis County neighborhoods may have mature trees, older roof penetrations, or layered ventilation history. Inspection notes should identify gutters, fascia, pipe collars, attic intake, and any brittle shingle fields before recommending repair or replacement.

A second Bountiful pass should note tree overhang, shaded valleys, snow melt lines, chimney crickets, attic exhaust placement, and gutter pitch. Those details are common on east-bench homes and keep the roof report focused on observable conditions instead of broad neighborhood assumptions.
