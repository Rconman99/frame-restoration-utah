How does lake-effect snow off the Great Salt Lake affect Clearfield roofs? +

Frame Restoration Utah installs ice-and-water shield at the eaves and valleys and specs underlayment rated for these conditions so Clearfield roofs shed meltwater instead of trapping it under the shingles.
