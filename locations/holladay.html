Holladay roof inspections should respect the city’s tree cover, older homes, and east-side storm exposure. We document branch abrasion, shaded-slope aging, chimney flashing, valley debris, hail marks, and wind-lifted tabs as separate findings.

Homes near Olympus, Cottonwood, and established residential streets can have multiple additions or roof transitions. The final notes should identify leak paths, attic ventilation, gutters, and roof-plane orientation before recommending repair, tarping, or replacement.

A second Holladay pass should identify branch rub, leaf-loaded valleys, chimney counterflashing, older roof additions, and attic intake restrictions. Mature tree cover can hide the source of leaks, so each finding should be documented separately.
