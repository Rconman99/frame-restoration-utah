Why does West Valley City have such a wide range of roof ages and shingle types? +

How does wind off the Oquirrh Mountains affect West Valley City roofs? +
West Valley City sits on the open western floor of the Salt Lake Valley at roughly 4,300 feet, directly downwind of the Oquirrh Mountains, so gusts funnel across the flat terrain with little to slow them. Those sustained winds lift and crease shingle tabs, loosen ridge caps, and work fasteners free along exposed edges and gable ends over time. Frame Restoration Utah counters this by sealing perimeters with starter strip, hand-nailing to manufacturer wind ratings, and inspecting flashing where the open valley exposure puts the most strain on a West Valley City roof.
