South Jordan is one of the youngest cities in the valley. Census ACS 2020–2024 estimates put the median year built at 2007, and only about 30% of homes were built before 2000. Most roofs here are still the ones the builder put on in the 2000s and 2010s, many of them in Daybreak and in the subdivisions that filled in between Bangerter Highway and the Mountain View Corridor.

The older part of town sits closer to Redwood Road and the Jordan River, where 1970s through 1990s homes mix with larger lots. More than four in five occupied South Jordan homes are owner-occupied, per the same estimates, so most storm calls come from owners deciding whether to repair or replace a roof they plan to keep for a long time.

Where South Jordan roofs give way

Most South Jordan storm damage falls into one of four patterns.

Hail on Builder Roofs

Bruising and granule loss on original builder-grade shingles, checked against dented vents, gutters, and soft metals.

Wind on Open Lots

Lifted ridge caps and creased tabs on homes with little shelter on the city's newer western edge.

Matching in HOA Neighborhoods

Repairs that have to match approved products and colors in Daybreak and other HOA-governed areas.

Open Roofs After a Storm

Emergency tarping to stop water, then the permanent repair. See emergency roof tarping.

How a South Jordan storm repair comes together

In a neighborhood with design rules, the paperwork matters as much as the shingles. Here is the order.

  1. 1. If the roof is open

    A tarp goes on to stop the water, fastened to hold until the permanent repair, as weather and safety allow.

  2. 2. Inspection and product match

    Each slope is checked and photographed, and the existing shingle line and color are identified so the repair can match the house and the street.

  3. 3. Design rules and permit

    Your HOA or design review requirements go into the written scope. A roof replacement also needs a South Jordan City building permit.

  4. 4. Repair or replacement

    The work is done to the approved product and color, with damaged vents and soft metals replaced along with the shingles.

How close has the 2026 hail come to South Jordan?

Hail came close to South Jordan at both ends of the 2026 storm season. Preliminary Storm Prediction Center reports list hail up to 1.75 inches in Taylorsville on the evening of June 25, 2026, and estimated quarter-size (1-inch) hail in neighboring West Jordan on the afternoon of September 18, 2026. Preliminary reports are not damage surveys and do not mean any South Jordan roof was hit.

Storms cross the south valley quickly. If you saw hail or heard it on the roof during either storm, do not assume the roof is fine just because there is no leak yet. A dated inspection ties any damage to a known storm.

Repairs in Daybreak and other HOA neighborhoods

Storm repairs in Daybreak and other South Jordan HOA neighborhoods usually have to match approved roofing products and colors. Homes in the same Daybreak village were often built close together with similar roofs, so one storm can mark a whole street, and a mismatched repair stands out.

Frame Restoration Utah records the existing shingle line and color in the scope and asks for your HOA or design review requirements before recommending a repair or replacement. South Jordan City requires its own building permit for roof replacements; see the South Jordan Building Department for current requirements.

Useful to have on hand

Any of these speeds up a South Jordan inspection.

  • The storm date and time, if you know it.
  • Ground-level photos of each side of the home before cleanup.
  • Photos of any dented gutters, vents, or window screens.
  • Your HOA or design review requirements for roofing, if any.
  • The year the home was built or the roof was installed.
  • Any interior leak spots or attic stains.

South Jordan storm questions

Is storm response available 24/7 in South Jordan? +
Yes. Emergency tarping and storm response are available 24/7 at 435-292-8802 when a storm leaves a South Jordan roof open or water is coming in. Inspections, estimates, and replacements are scheduled during business hours (Mon–Fri 8am–7pm, Sat 8am–6pm, Sun closed).
Does my Daybreak HOA need to approve a storm repair? +
Daybreak and other South Jordan HOA neighborhoods usually require approved roofing products and colors. Check your HOA or design review requirements; we record them and the existing product in the written scope.
Is my original builder roof more likely to be damaged by hail? +
Not necessarily, but many South Jordan roofs are original builder installs, and damage on a roof that age is still worth documenting. An inspection separates hail bruising from normal wear.
Do I need a permit to replace my roof in South Jordan? +
South Jordan City requires a building permit for roof replacements. Check the South Jordan Building Department for current requirements.
