Orem’s housing came in two big waves. Census ACS 2020–2024 estimates count about one in five Orem homes as built in the 1970s and another one in five in the 1990s, with a median year built of 1989. The seventies homes lean toward ramblers and split-levels; the nineties homes lean toward two-story plans with a main-floor laundry and a basement that was finished later.

About four in ten occupied Orem homes are rented, per the same estimates, a share shaped in part by Utah Valley University. So the person calling might be the owner, a tenant in a basement unit, or a landlord who lives across town, and the scope is written so each of them can follow it.

The usual suspects in an Orem home

Orem water damage tends to start in one of four places.

Laundry and appliances

Washer hoses, dishwasher lines, and refrigerator supply lines, often on a main floor above finished space.

Water heaters

Basement tanks that rust through next to a family room or bedroom.

Frozen pipes and sprinkler lines

Exterior-wall plumbing and sprinkler lines near the foundation that split in a hard freeze.

Roof leaks

Ceiling water after wind or hail, with the roof repair on the same scope.

From the phone call to the finished room

Every Orem water job follows the same four stages, though no two take the same amount of time.

  1. 1. Hour one

    Water confirmed off, electrical hazards checked, and wet rooms photographed. Extraction starts in the lowest wet room, because that is where the water is collecting.

  2. 2. Day one

    A moisture meter traces the path from the source down through the floor system and into the basement, with each reading marked. Wet pad, swollen baseboard, and drywall that wicked water are removed at measured lines, and the subfloor under a main-floor laundry is checked from below.

  3. 3. Drying

    Dehumidifiers and air movers run on both floors if the water crossed between them. Return visits read the same marked points until the numbers say dry.

  4. 4. Rebuild

    Subfloor and underlayment if needed, then drywall, insulation, trim, flooring, and paint, in a written rebuild scope kept separate from the emergency work.

What happens when water runs from an Orem main floor to the basement?

In an Orem two-story with a main-floor laundry, a failed washer hose does not stay in the laundry room. The water soaks the subfloor, runs along the joists, and comes out through the basement ceiling, sometimes a room or two away from the source. Water damage restoration in Orem in that case means checking both floors and the space between them, not just the carpet that looks wet.

That is why the first-day readings follow the water’s path from top to bottom. Where the subfloor or the basement ceiling is wet, it is opened or dried in place based on the readings, and each decision is photographed.

Bench lots and spring runoff

Much of east Orem sits on benches below Mount Timpanogos, which are old Lake Bonneville shorelines, and the Provo River comes out of Provo Canyon between Orem and Provo. On sloped bench lots, snowmelt and summer storms run toward foundations and window wells, and a sprinkler line that was not drained before a hard freeze can split right next to the house.

If you live near the river or on a steep lot, look the address up on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center. For water damage restoration in Orem where the water came in from outside, see flooded basement cleanup.

Before you call, and while you wait

Shut off the water at the main valve or at the failed fixture, then call 435-292-8802; Orem water and flood emergencies are answered 24/7. Keep everyone out of rooms where water is near outlets, light fixtures, or the electrical panel.

Take photos before moving anything, note when it started, and move electronics and paperwork out of the wet area. If you rent, tell the owner or property manager as soon as the water is off.

A short checklist for Orem owners and renters

Do these while you wait, if it is safe.

  • Water off at the main or the fixture.
  • Photos of each wet room, upstairs and down.
  • The time the leak started or was found.
  • Electronics and paperwork out of the wet area.
  • Everyone away from wet outlets, fixtures, and the panel.
  • The owner told, if you rent, and insurance details handy.
