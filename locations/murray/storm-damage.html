Murray's storm story is the inverse of the bench cities. There's no canyon mouth here aiming wind at the rooftops — what Murray gets is the weather everyone shares, delivered flat and wide. When a hail cell crosses the valley, Murray's central grid between State Street and the Jordan River puts block after block under the same swath. One afternoon cell can bruise hundreds of roofs from Fashion Place to Murray Park, all with similar damage signatures.

The complication is underfoot, not overhead. Murray built out in the 1950s through 70s, and a meaningful share of its roofs carry overlay history — new shingles laid over old in the era when that was code-legal and common. Overlaid roofs take hail worse (the soft under-layer lets mats flex and fracture) and repair worse (fasteners and flashing never sit right through two layers). Honest storm work in Murray starts with establishing what's actually on the deck.

Murray Storm Damage FAQs

Why does Murray see so much hail damage compared to wind damage? +
Geography. Murray sits at the center of the valley floor, away from the canyon mouths that funnel destructive winds onto the east bench. What crosses Murray instead are the hail cells themselves — when a spring or summer storm trains up the valley, Murray's flat, central position puts entire neighborhoods under the swath at once. After a significant hailfall, we routinely inspect whole streets in Murray where every roof took similar impact.
My Murray home has an older roof — does that change storm repair? +
Often, yes. Much of Murray was built in the 1950s-70s, and some of those roofs carry overlays from the era when layering new shingles over old was standard. Hail hits an overlaid roof harder — the soft layer underneath lets the mat flex and fracture — and repairs on overlays rarely hold. Our free inspection establishes what's actually on the roof, because the right answer for a single-layer roof and an overlay are different conversations.
Will insurance cover hail damage on my Murray roof? +
Sudden hail and wind damage is covered by most homeowner policies.
