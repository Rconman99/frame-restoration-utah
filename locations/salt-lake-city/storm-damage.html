What storm damage looks like on Salt Lake City roofs

Frame Restoration Utah sees four recurring kinds of storm damage on Salt Lake City homes and small buildings.

Hail on Older Asphalt

Bruised and granule-stripped shingles on aging roofs, plus dented vents, gutters, and soft metals that confirm the hail's size and direction.

Wind-Lifted Shingles

Creased or missing tabs on ridges and rakes, common on east bench homes and older roofs with weak sealant strips.

Low-Slope Additions

Ponding, torn seams, and failed flashing on the flat or low-slope rear additions many older Salt Lake City homes have.

Storm-Opened Roofs

Emergency tarping when a limb, gust, or hail leaves the roof open, then a permanent repair. See emergency roof tarping.

How does hail reach Salt Lake City, and how big has it been lately?

Hail reaches Salt Lake City mostly in late-spring and summer thunderstorms that cross the valley, and 2026 brought large hail very close to the city. Preliminary Storm Prediction Center reports for the evening of June 25, 2026 list hail up to 1.75 inches in Taylorsville and up to about 1.5 inches in Holladay, both a short drive from Salt Lake City neighborhoods.

Hail does not have to be golf-ball sized to matter on a Salt Lake City roof. On 30- and 40-year-old shingles, smaller stones can still break the mat or knock loose the granules that protect it. Frame Restoration Utah separates fresh hail bruising from age-related wear before recommending anything, because an old roof that is simply worn out needs a different conversation than a storm-damaged one.

Which Salt Lake City neighborhoods need extra care after a storm?

Salt Lake City neighborhoods with the oldest roofs and the most roof add-ons need the most careful storm inspections. In the Avenues and Capitol Hill, steep pitches, dormers, and older chimneys create flashing joints that storms pry open. In Sugar House, Liberty Wells, and 9th and 9th, bungalows often have a low-slope rear addition tied into a steeper main roof. On the east bench, higher exposure means more wind on ridges and rakes. West-side neighborhoods such as Rose Park and Glendale have many mid-century ramblers where an older overlay can hide decking damage.

Yalecrest and other Salt Lake City local historic districts can add a review step when a storm repair changes roofing material or visible details. Frame Restoration Utah notes the existing materials in the scope and points owners to Salt Lake City Historic Preservation so the review question is answered before work starts.

What to have ready before you call about Salt Lake City storm damage

Having these items ready makes a Salt Lake City storm inspection faster and the written scope more accurate.

  • The date and approximate time of the storm.
  • Photos from the ground of each side of the home, taken before anything is moved.
  • Any interior leak spots, with photos of ceilings or attic stains.
  • The roof's approximate age and any past repairs you know about.
  • Whether the home is in a local historic district (for example, Yalecrest or the Avenues).
  • If you are a landlord or property manager, the best contact for tenant access.
