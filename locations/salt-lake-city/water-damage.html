Salt Lake City's housing stock ranges from century-old homes in the Avenues, Sugar House, and the east bench to newer construction on the west side and across the valley. Older homes bring galvanized and aging supply lines, unfinished basements, and roofs with years of patched flashing. Newer homes bring finished basements full of drywall and carpet that soak up water fast. Both need the same thing when water gets in: fast extraction and careful drying.

Winter inversions and cold snaps freeze pipes in exterior walls and crawlspaces, spring snowmelt from the benches pushes groundwater toward foundations, and summer storms test roofs and gutters. We see all of it across the valley.

Do you respond to water damage in Salt Lake City 24/7? +
Yes. Our restoration team answers water and flood emergencies 24/7 at 435-292-8802, anywhere in the Salt Lake Valley. Emergency tarping and storm response are also available 24/7. What causes the most water damage in Salt Lake City homes? +
Frozen and burst pipes during cold snaps, failed water heaters and appliance supply lines, roof leaks and ice dams in older homes, and basements that take on water during snowmelt or heavy storms.
Do you have an office in Salt Lake City? +
No. In the Salt Lake Valley we are a service-area business and come to your property. Our office is at 142 S Main St, Heber City, UT 84032.
Can you fix the roof leak and the interior damage? +
Yes. Frame Restoration Utah is a licensed roofing contractor, so we can repair the roof leak that caused the damage and restore the interior under one scope.
Do you handle sewage backups in Salt Lake City? +
No. Sewage backup cleanup is outside our scope. If a sewer backup is involved, we will tell you so you can bring in a sewage cleanup specialist.
