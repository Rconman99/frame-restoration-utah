What sends water into a Millcreek house

Four things cause most of the water trouble in an older east-side home.

Window wells and grading

Summer cloudbursts and spring melt fill window wells and run toward older foundations on sloped lots.

Aging supply lines

Decades-old supply lines, valves, and water heaters in basements that were finished after the fact.

Frozen pipes

Lines in exterior walls, crawlspaces, and attached garages that split when cold air settles in on a still winter night.

Roof leaks

Low-slope rambler roofs and add-ons that leak at the transitions. See storm damage roof repair in Millcreek.

How a Millcreek water job unfolds

Four stages, in this order, whatever the size of the job.

  1. 1. First hour: stop it and look

    Water off or diverted, electrical hazards checked, and every wet surface photographed. If the source is a window well, the well is pumped and the water outside is directed away before the inside work starts. Extraction then pulls standing water off the floor and out of the carpet.

  2. 2. First day: map and open up

    Meters on every wall, floor, and built-in, with each reading marked. Older basements sometimes have paneling over furring strips on concrete, which traps water behind it; panels come off where the readings say so, along with wet pad and drywall.

  3. 3. Drying

    Dehumidifiers and air movers run against the marked readings. Concrete foundation walls in older homes hold moisture, so drying is finished when the numbers say it is, not when the surface feels dry.

  4. 4. Rebuild

    Insulation, drywall, trim, flooring, and paint, written as a repair scope separate from the emergency work. If a window well or drain was the source, the scope notes what was found so it can be fixed before the room is finished again.

Millcreek sits where Mill Creek comes out of its canyon onto the valley floor, and the east side of the city climbs the foothills below Mount Olympus. Old pipes behind new walls

Many Millcreek basements were finished long after the plumbing went in, so supply lines and drain connections often run behind newer drywall with no access panel. When one of those fails, water damage restoration in Millcreek means opening the wall carefully, finding the wet cavity with a meter, and leaving a clean opening.

If the failed line needs a plumber, the opening is left so the repair can be made through it, and the drying schedule is planned around that visit. Nothing gets closed up until the readings say the cavity is dry.

First steps when water shows up

Shut off the main water valve if a pipe or appliance is the source. If the water is coming from outside, redirect a downspout or bail a window well only if you can do it safely. Then call 435-292-8802; Millcreek water and flood emergencies are answered 24/7. Stay away from water near outlets, cords, or the panel.

Before moving anything, take photos and note the time. Lift rugs, boxes, and electronics off the floor, and if the room is rented, let both the tenant and the owner know what happened.
