After a Storm Hits Millcreek

  • Limb on the roof? Stay out of the room below it and call 435-292-8802 — punctured decks on older homes are structural, not cosmetic.
  • Photograph from the ground: displaced shingles, leaning limbs, granule piles at downspouts, dented vents.
  • Check the attic if it's safe — daylight through the deck or wet insulation means tarp now, not next week.
  • Book the free inspection even if it "looks fine." On 50-year-old decking, wind-broken shingle seals leak through paths newer roofs don't have.

Millcreek Storm Damage FAQs

What makes Millcreek's storm damage different from other Salt Lake Valley cities? +
Two things: the age of the roofs and the size of the trees. Millcreek's neighborhoods — Canyon Rim, East Millcreek, Evergreen — are among the valley's oldest east-bench communities, full of 1950s-70s ramblers and split-levels whose decking has absorbed seventy winters. Above them stands one of the valley's heaviest mature canopies. When Millcreek Canyon funnels a wind event across the bench, old limbs meet old roofs, and the damage runs deeper than shingles.
How does Millcreek Canyon affect wind damage on my roof? +
Millcreek Canyon behaves like its Cottonwood neighbors: a pressure gradient across the Wasatch accelerates air down-canyon and discharges it across the east bench. Homes on the upper avenues near the canyon mouth and along the Olympus Cove flank catch the strongest gusts. The signature damage is wind uplift — creased shingles and lifted tabs along windward edges — which breaks the adhesive seal invisibly and turns into leaks one or two storms later.
Will my insurer cover storm repairs on an older Millcreek roof? +
Sudden storm damage is covered by most homeowner policies regardless of roof age — but adjusters scrutinize older roofs harder, separating storm damage from wear. That's why documentation quality decides outcomes in Millcreek. Frame Restoration Utah photographs and labels each finding, prepares a detailed line-item scope your adjuster can read, and meets the adjuster on the roof. the roof claim file stays between you and your insurer — we document the damage, we don't adjust — and your required out-of-pocket amount is yours to pay under Utah law.
A storm exposed bigger problems with my old Millcreek roof — repair or replace? +
It depends on what the storm revealed. If the deck is sound and the field shingles have life left, a targeted repair is honest money well spent. If the storm peeled back shingles to show rotted decking, multiple layers from old overlays, or brittle mats that crumble at a touch, repairs become a bandage on a roof that's done. Our free inspection gives you the straight answer either way — including "this roof is fine" when it is.

Millcreek storm inspection notes

Millcreek storm inspections should include the east-side mix of mature trees, older homes, additions, and canyon-influenced weather. We document lifted tabs, hail bruising, branch abrasion, valley debris, chimney flashing, and soft-metal marks independently.

The report should also note whether a leak starts at a roof penetration, sidewall transition, gutter backflow, or aged sealant. That distinction helps keep emergency tarping, repair, and replacement recommendations honest after a Salt Lake Valley storm.

Millcreek follow-up note

A second storm pass should include branch impact, older addition seams, roof-to-wall flashing, low-slope porch tie-ins, and gutter overflow near mature trees. Millcreek leak diagnosis is strongest when drainage and impact evidence are kept separate.
