Midvale roof inspection notes

Midvale inspections should account for the city’s central-valley roof mix: older homes, townhomes, mixed-use buildings, and freeway-adjacent corridors. We document pipe boots, flat transitions, valleys, gutters, parapet edges, and storm evidence separately.

Because some Midvale leaks come from aging drainage rather than fresh impact, the report should distinguish hail bruising, wind creases, old sealant, flashing gaps, and interior ceiling stains. That makes the repair recommendation easier to verify.

Midvale follow-up note

A second Midvale pass should record evaporative-cooler stands, low-slope porch roofs, parapet edges, scuppers, and garage transitions. Those details are common on central-valley properties and can determine whether a leak is a flashing repair or broader roof issue.
