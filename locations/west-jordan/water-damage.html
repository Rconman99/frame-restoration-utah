What happens once the crew is downstairs

A West Jordan basement water job runs in four stages.

  1. 1. Stop it and see it

    Water off, electrical hazards checked, and every wet room photographed before anything moves. Then extraction pulls the standing water off the slab and out of the carpet.

  2. 2. Find the edges

    A moisture meter on every wall, floor, and stair stringer shows where the water went, and each reading is marked. Carpet pad that soaked through, swollen baseboard, and drywall that wicked water are cut out at a measured line.

  3. 3. Dry

    Dehumidifiers and air movers run in the basement. Basements are cool, and cool air dries slowly, so the return visits read the same marked points until the numbers say dry.

  4. 4. Rebuild

    Insulation, drywall, baseboard, carpet and pad, and paint, written up as a repair scope that is kept separate from the emergency work.

What causes most water damage in West Jordan homes?

Most water damage in West Jordan homes starts inside: failed water heaters, appliance supply lines, and pipes that freeze during cold snaps. In homes built in the 1980s and 1990s, the original water heater and supply hoses may be well past their expected life, and a finished basement turns a small leak into a big drying job.

Outside water matters too. Summer thunderstorms can overwhelm gutters and window wells, and a storm-damaged roof can leak into an attic for days before a ceiling stain shows. Because Frame Restoration Utah is also a licensed roofing contractor, the roof leak and the interior can go on one scope.

First ten minutes after you find the water

Stop the water first, at the main shutoff or, if the water heater is the source, at the valve on its cold supply line. Then call 435-292-8802; we answer water and flood emergencies in West Jordan 24/7. If water is near outlets, cords, or the electrical panel, stay out of the area.

While you wait, take photos before moving anything, note when the leak started, and carry valuables and electronics upstairs. Skip the household shop vacuum in standing water near anything electrical.

Are homes near the Jordan River at higher flood risk?

Some can be. The Jordan River runs along West Jordan's eastern edge, and FEMA flood maps show mapped flood zones along parts of the river corridor. Whether a specific address is in a mapped zone depends on the address, so check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your property.

Most West Jordan basement water we see comes from plumbing failures and storm runoff rather than the river. The drying process is the same either way: remove standing water, map the moisture, remove what cannot be dried, and dry the structure to documented readings.

Which pipes freeze first in a West Jordan home?

The pipes that freeze first in West Jordan homes are usually outdoor hose bibs, supply lines in exterior walls, lines through unheated garages, and plumbing near basement window wells or rim joists where the insulation is thin. A frozen line often splits quietly and only starts leaking once it thaws, so the water can run for hours before anyone notices.

Before a hard freeze, disconnect garden hoses, shut off and drain exterior spigots that have an inside valve, and open cabinet doors on outside walls. If a line has already split, shut off the main and call; we extract the water, find every wet wall cavity with the meter, and dry the structure before any drywall goes back up.

Small steps that make a big difference

These protect the house and make the West Jordan water damage scope more accurate.

  • Shut off the water at the main valve, or at the fixture if you can.
  • Take photos of the water and the damage before moving anything.
  • Note the time the leak started or was found.
  • Move valuables, electronics, and documents to a dry area.
  • Keep people and pets away from water near outlets or the electrical panel.
  • Have your insurance policy information handy, if you plan to share the documentation.
