West Jordan is a city of two roof generations. Census ACS 2020–2024 estimates put the median home at 1995, with about 62% of housing units built before 2000. The older half sits mostly on the east side, between the Jordan River and Redwood Road, in 1970s and 1980s ramblers and split-levels. Newer subdivisions climb west toward Bangerter Highway, the Mountain View Corridor, and the Oquirrh foothills.

Those two generations fail differently in a storm. An original 1980s roof may already be near the end of its life when hail arrives, while a 2010s builder roof is more likely to show isolated hail bruising and wind-lifted tabs. Frame Restoration Utah inspects for both, so a West Jordan homeowner gets a scope that fits the roof actually on the house.

What storm damage looks like on West Jordan roofs

Frame Restoration Utah sees four recurring kinds of storm damage on West Jordan homes.

Hail Bruising

Soft, dark impact marks and granule loss on shingles, confirmed against dented gutters, vents, and window screens.

West-Side Wind Exposure

Lifted and creased tabs on ridges and rakes, especially on newer homes on the more open western edge of the city.

Aging East-Side Roofs

Older east-side roofs where hail speeds up existing wear, and a repair may not be practical.

Open Roofs After a Storm

Emergency tarping to stop water, then the permanent repair. See emergency roof tarping.

Did hail hit West Jordan in September 2026?

Yes. A preliminary Storm Prediction Center report for the afternoon of September 18, 2026 describes estimated quarter-size (1-inch) hail in West Jordan, based on a social media video, with similar 1-inch reports in neighboring Taylorsville minutes later. Preliminary reports are not a damage survey, and they do not mean any particular roof was hit.

Hail that size can bruise shingles, crack older vents, and dent gutters without causing a leak right away. If your West Jordan neighborhood saw hail that afternoon, a documented inspection now is better than waiting for a leak, because it ties the findings to a known storm date.

Why do West Jordan storms often hit the west side first?

West Jordan storms often hit the west side first because many summer thunderstorms build over the Oquirrh Mountains and move east across the valley. Neighborhoods closest to the foothills, such as those near the Mountain View Corridor, can take the leading edge of hail and outflow wind before the storm reaches the Jordan River side of the city.

That does not make the east side safe. Storms also form or strengthen over the valley itself, and older east-side roofs have less margin left. Frame Restoration Utah treats every West Jordan address on its own record rather than assuming a whole neighborhood is or is not damaged.

What to have ready before you call about West Jordan storm damage

These items make a West Jordan storm inspection faster and the scope more accurate.

  • The storm date (for example, the afternoon of September 18, 2026) and the time if you know it.
  • Ground-level photos of each side of the home before anything is cleaned up.

  • Any interior leak spots or attic stains.

  • Your HOA's roofing requirements, if your subdivision has them.
