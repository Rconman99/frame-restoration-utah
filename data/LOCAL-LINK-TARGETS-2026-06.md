## 1. Strategy

**True-local links over link volume.** The research finding driving this list: *one link
from a genuinely local community page, local newspaper, or local event/sponsor page does
more for Maps/local-pack and AI-answer visibility than ten generic SEO-blog links or a
pile of paid directory submissions.* The value comes from **local proof + entity
corroboration + referral traffic**, not from link count.

- **Editorial / community proof.** A mention in the *Park Record* or a Chamber member
  listing tells Google (and AI answer engines) that a real Wasatch Front business named
  Frame Restoration exists, operates here, and is recognized by trusted local entities.
  That is *entity corroboration* — the same NAP (Name / Address / Phone) and brand
  appearing on independent, locally-relevant, editorially-controlled sites.
- **Why this supports SEO/AEO.** Local-pack ranking weights proximity + prominence;
  prominence is built from locally-relevant links and consistent citations. AI Overviews
  / ChatGPT / Perplexity disproportionately cite locally-trusted sources (news, chambers,
  associations) when answering "best roofer/restoration in [city]." These targets are
  exactly those source types.
- **Why referral traffic matters more than link volume.** A sponsorship link on a Heber
  Swiss Days page or a Chamber profile can send *actual homeowners* who are local and
  high-intent. A referral that books a roof inspection is worth more than 50 links nobody
  clicks. **We will measure success by referral traffic + leads, not by link count or
  keyword rank** (rank is contaminated by other AEO work already in flight).
- **Why reciprocal exchanges are REJECTED.** Organized "link to me and I'll link to you"
  rings are the single highest-penalty pattern in 2026 SEO. Google's SpamBrain devalues
  identified exchange patterns in *days* (real-time since the Aug 2025 spam update), and
  exact-match reciprocal ratios concentrated among a small cluster of domains are one of
  its highest-confidence manipulation signals. Manual actions cut organic traffic 50–90%
  with 6–12 month recovery. **We build relationships whose links are an editorial
  byproduct — never a swap.** (Sources in Rejected Tactics.)

**The line, stated once:** A link is legitimate here if Frame would want the
relationship *even if the link were nofollow* — because it brings local trust, a real
audience, or referral business. If the only reason to pursue it is the link, it is out.

## 4. Rejected Tactics (so future agents understand the line)

These are **explicitly out of scope** and must never be recommended or executed:

1. **Reciprocal link exchanges / "link rings."** "Link to me and I'll link to you" among
   a known group is the highest-penalty 2026 pattern — SpamBrain devalues it in days, and
   exact-match reciprocal ratios in a small domain cluster are a top manipulation signal.
   *Reject.*
2. **Wikipedia city-page citation "hack."** Adding Frame as a reference on a city's
   Wikipedia page. Wikipedia external links are `nofollow` (pass no link equity), it
   violates Wikipedia's conflict-of-interest/external-link policies, and the viral "moves
   rankings more than 100 citations" claim is false. *Reject.*
3. **Bulk paid directory submissions** ("500 directories for $20" / Fiverr packages).
   Low-quality, often spammy/foreign directories; dilutes the profile and risks
   association with link farms. *Reject.*
4. **Automated AI outreach spam.** Mass-generated outreach emails. Over-automated outreach
   *is* the spam pattern; even AI-SEO practitioners say the agent's job is "fewer, better"
   emails and that relationship-building stays human. Use AI for *discovery/scoring only*,
   never auto-send. *Reject (as a tactic; human-approved outreach is a separate gated step).*
5. **Fake/ghost sponsorships.** Claiming sponsorship of an event or org Frame did not
   actually fund, or buying a "sponsor link" with no real sponsorship. *Reject.*
6. **Fake office / location proof.** Spinning up fake addresses, virtual offices, or
   PO-box "locations" to manufacture local citations in cities Frame doesn't truly serve.
   This is also a GBP guideline violation. *Reject.*
7. **(Bonus) Private Blog Networks (PBNs), paid "link insertions," blog-comment links.**
   Same family as the above. *Reject.*
