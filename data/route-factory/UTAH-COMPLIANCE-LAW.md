# Utah Roofing Compliance — Legal Basis for `compliance-words.json`

**Researched 2026-05-30** (primary sources: le.utah.gov, commerce.utah.gov/dopl). This file is the
provenance trail for the forbidden-word + saturation rules enforced by
`scripts/audit-compliance-words.mjs`. It replaces the Texas §4102.163 / TDI basis — **none of which
applies in Utah.** Do not cite "Texas §4102" or "TDI" anywhere in Utah copy or rules.

## 1. Unlicensed insurance adjusting (the Utah "negotiate" basis)

Utah has **no** Texas-style statute naming roofers and banning "negotiate." Instead, claim
negotiation/settlement is a **licensed act**, and an unlicensed roofer performing it violates the
licensing requirement.

- **§ 31A-26-102(6):** "'Insurance adjusting' … means directing or conducting the investigation,
  **negotiation**, or settlement of a claim under an insurance policy …"
- **§ 31A-26-201(1)(a):** "a person may not perform, offer to perform, or solicit the opportunity to
  perform an act of insurance adjusting without a valid license…" (roofers are not an exempt category)
- **NEW 2026 (Ch. 45):** §31A-26-403.1 and §31A-26-405(16) name "roofing company" explicitly
  (assignment limits; PA may not let a roofing company obtain the insured's signature).

→ Forbidden: "negotiate [your/the] claim," "settle your claim," "adjust your claim," "we handle your
claim," "we're your public adjuster."

## 2. Deductible-rebate ban — Insured Homeowners Protection Act (the HB 2102 analog)

- **§ 13-50-302:** "A residential contractor may not rebate or offer to rebate any portion of an
  insurance deductible as an inducement to the sale of a good or service."
- **§ 13-50-303** mandates a 14-pt notice that the **homeowner is personally responsible for the
  deductible.** "Rebate" expressly includes *granting any allowance, offering any discount against
  fees, or paying the policyholder the deductible amount.*

→ Forbidden inducement phrasings: "waive/cover/eat/absorb your deductible," "we pay your deductible,"
"no deductible," "deductible discount," "free roof," "no out-of-pocket," "$0 out of pocket."
→ NOT forbidden: the word "deductible" alone — the law *requires* stating the homeowner owes it.

## 3. DOPL licensing

- Body: **Division of Professional Licensing (DOPL)** (formerly Division of Occupational & Professional
  Licensing), Utah Dept. of Commerce.
- Act: **Utah Code Title 58, Ch. 55** (Construction Trades Licensing Act), §58-55-301.
- Roofing classification: **S280 — Roofing Contractor.** Don't assert a license/classification not held.

## 4. Public adjuster = the reserved licensed role

- **§ 31A-26-102(9):** a "public adjuster" engages in insurance adjusting as a representative of
  insureds. **§31A-26-312(1):** an adjuster may not participate in the repair/reconstruction of property
  they adjust (the firewall — runs adjuster→not-contractor, the inverse of TX §4102.163).

## Safe-harbor framing (legally fine)

Describing the roofing work; offering to **inspect/document** storm or hail damage; **meeting the
adjuster on-site**; providing **estimates/scopes**; and stating the **homeowner is responsible for the
deductible.**

## Caveats

§31A-26-403.1 and -405 are brand-new (2026 General Session); verify effective dates before relying on
them in legal copy. Penalty grades not pulled. Use "Division of Professional Licensing (DOPL)" as the
primary agency name.
