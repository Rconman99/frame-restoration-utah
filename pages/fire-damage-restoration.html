Even a small kitchen fire can spread smoke residue through a whole house. Soot settles on walls and ceilings, smoke works into HVAC ductwork and insulation, and the water used to put out the fire soaks into floors and wall cavities. Left alone, residue keeps smelling and staining, and wet materials can grow mold.

Frame Restoration Utah works through fire damage in a set order so nothing is missed: secure the structure, remove what cannot be saved, clean what can, deal with the water, treat odor at the source, and rebuild.

Fire and Smoke Damage Restoration FAQs for Utah

Is fire damage restoration available 24/7?

Fire and smoke damage restoration is scheduled during business hours (Mon–Fri 8am–7pm, Sat 8am–6pm, Sun closed). Our 24/7 line covers water and flood emergencies, emergency tarping, and storm response, so if a fire has left the roof open we can tarp it right away, and if firefighting water is still soaking the structure, call 435-292-8802 and we can treat that as a water emergency.

When can I go back into my home after a fire?

Do not re-enter until the fire department or the appropriate authority says it is safe. Fire can weaken floors, roofs, and stairs, and smoke residue can be hazardous. The U.S. Fire Administration's after-fire guidance is a good checklist for the first days.

Can smoke odor really be removed?

In most cases, yes, but only if the residue causing it is removed. Smoke travels into HVAC ducts, insulation, wall cavities, and soft materials. We clean residue at the source, treat the odor, and deodorize ductwork and soft surfaces rather than masking the smell.

Do you handle the rebuild after fire damage?

Yes. One team handles cleanup, smoke and odor treatment, and structural repairs, including the roof. That keeps the scope, schedule, and documentation in one place.

Will you work with my insurance company?

We document conditions with photos, moisture readings, and a written scope you can share with your insurer. Coverage decisions stay between you and your insurance company; we document and restore, we do not adjust claims.
