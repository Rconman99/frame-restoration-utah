Frequently Asked Questions

Will solar panels damage my roof or void the warranty?

Not when they're installed correctly. We use flashed, watertight mounts and integrate the array with the roof system rather than just bolting through shingles. Because we're a roofing contractor first, every penetration is sealed to roofing standards and your workmanship warranty stays intact.

Should I replace my roof before installing solar?

If your roof is within a few years of the end of its life, yes — replace it first. Removing and reinstalling panels later to redo a roof is costly. We inspect the roof's remaining lifespan before any solar install and tell you honestly whether to reroof first.

How does net metering work with my Utah utility?

If your utility offers net metering, the excess power your panels produce is credited against the power you draw at night or in winter. Terms vary by provider and change over time, so confirm the current net-metering or export-credit program directly with your utility before sizing a system.

Do solar batteries keep my power on during an outage?

Only if your system includes battery backup configured for islanding. A standard grid-tied array shuts off during an outage for safety. Adding storage and the right inverter lets essential circuits keep running through Wasatch Front windstorms and winter outages.
