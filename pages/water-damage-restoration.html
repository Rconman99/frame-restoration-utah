Water damage gets worse by the hour. Drywall wicks water upward, wood flooring cups, insulation holds moisture against framing, and mold can start growing on wet materials within a day or two. The goal of the first visit is simple: stop the water, get it out, and start drying before secondary damage sets in.

Utah homes see a predictable set of water problems: pipes that freeze and split during cold snaps, supply lines and water heaters that fail without warning, ice dams that push meltwater under shingles, and roof leaks after wind or hail. Because Frame Restoration Utah is also a licensed roofing contractor, we can trace a leak to its source, fix the roof, and restore the interior with one accountable team.

What We Do on a Water Damage Job

Emergency Water Extraction

Standing water removed with extraction equipment so drying can start. Available 24/7 for water emergencies.

Moisture Mapping

Moisture readings and thermal imaging to find water hiding in walls, subfloors, and ceilings, recorded at the start and throughout drying.

Structural Drying

Dehumidifiers and air movers placed for the affected rooms, with readings checked until materials reach a dry standard.

Repairs and Rebuild

Drywall, insulation, flooring, trim, and roof repairs once the structure is dry, under one written scope.

Common Causes We Respond To

  • Burst and frozen pipes: Cold snaps along the Wasatch Front and in mountain towns split pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, and garages.
  • Appliance and fixture leaks: Water heaters, washing machine hoses, dishwasher and refrigerator lines, and toilet supply lines.
  • Roof leaks and ice dams: Water coming through ceilings after wind, hail, heavy snow, or ice dams. We can repair the roof leak as well as the interior.
  • Flooded basements: Snowmelt, storm runoff, and failed sump pumps. See our flooded basement and flood cleanup page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you really answer water damage calls 24/7?

Yes. Our restoration team answers water and flood emergencies 24/7 at 435-292-8802, including nights, weekends, and holidays. The 24/7 line is for active water and flood emergencies; roofing, fire and smoke, mold, and siding work is scheduled during business hours (Mon–Fri 8am–7pm, Sat 8am–6pm, Sun closed).

What should I do first when water is coming in?

Make sure everyone is safe, keep people away from wet electrical outlets and appliances, and shut off the water at the main valve if a pipe or appliance is the source. Then call us. Take photos of the affected rooms before anything is moved so the damage record is complete.

How do you know when my home is actually dry?

We take moisture readings in the affected walls, floors, and framing at the start, check them throughout drying, and keep drying equipment running until materials reach a dry standard. Surfaces can feel dry while the material behind them is still wet, which is why we rely on readings instead of touch.

Can you repair the roof leak that caused the water damage?

Yes. Frame Restoration Utah is a licensed roofing contractor, so when water damage starts at the roof we can repair the leak source and restore the interior damage it caused. One team handles both so nothing falls between trades.

Will my insurance cover water damage restoration?

That depends on your policy and the cause of the water. We document conditions with photos, moisture readings, and a written scope you can share with your insurer. Coverage decisions stay between you and your insurance company; we document and restore, we do not adjust claims.

Do you handle sewage backups?

No. Sewage backup cleanup is outside our scope. If a sewer or septic backup is involved, we will tell you so you can bring in a sewage cleanup specialist; we can still help with clean-water damage and related repairs.
