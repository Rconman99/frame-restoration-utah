Hail Damage Repair

Utah sits in the heart of the Wasatch Front hail belt, where summer storms routinely produce golf-ball-sized hail that destroys shingles, dents gutters, and cracks ridge caps. Frame Restoration Utah specializes in identifying hail damage that untrained eyes miss — the subtle bruising and granule loss that carrier representatives may look for during inspections.

After a hailstorm, time matters. Policy notice rules, filing windows, deductibles, exclusions, and covered scope are carrier-specific, so homeowners should document conditions early and read their policy before assuming a deadline or coverage outcome. We provide free hail damage assessments and clear documentation you can use in that process.

Wind & Snow Damage

Canyon winds along the Wasatch Front can gust above 100 mph, tearing shingles clean off, lifting flashing, and exposing your roof deck to moisture. Heavy snow loads — especially in areas like Park City, Heber City, and the benches above Salt Lake — create ice dams that force water underneath shingles and into your attic.

Whether it is wind-lifted shingles, crushed vents from snow weight, or ice dam leaks dripping through your ceiling, our crews know exactly how to assess and repair these Utah-specific problems quickly and permanently.

Source-Backed Storm Damage Checks

  • 2-inch steel ball at 20 feet is the published UL 2218 Class 4 impact-test reference GAF describes for impact-rated shingles. See GAF Class 4 shingles.
  • 24 inches past the interior wall line is the IRC ice-barrier reference for applicable ice-dam areas. See IRC R905.1.2 ice barriers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do immediately after a hailstorm in Utah?

Document damage with ground-level photos, avoid climbing on the roof, and call Frame Restoration Utah at 435-292-8802 for a free storm-damage inspection within 24 hours. We meet your carrier representative on-site to make sure every piece of damage is captured.

Does insurance cover hail damage to my roof?

Coverage depends on your policy language, cause of loss, deductible, exclusions, and carrier findings. Frame Restoration Utah documents visible roof conditions with photos and written notes so your carrier review has clearer evidence.

How long do I have to file a storm-damage claim in Utah?

Filing and notice windows are policy-specific. Do not wait to document damage, but read your policy or talk with your carrier before assuming a universal Utah deadline. Call 435-292-8802 to start the inspection process.

Is storm-damage inspection free?

Yes. Every Frame Restoration Utah storm-damage inspection is 100% free and no-obligation across the Wasatch Front. We'll give you an honest assessment of whether you have a roof claim file worth filing before you call your carrier.
