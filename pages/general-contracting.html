Project Controls That Protect Utah Owners

The same ownerless quality loop we use for roofing applies to remodeling: written scope, permit path, change-order rules, photo documentation, and one accountable closeout packet.

  • 1. License and insurance verification: Utah's Division of Consumer Protection home-improvement guidance recommends confirming contractor license, insurance, references, and written contract terms before work starts.
  • 2. Permit path: Additions, structural changes, electrical, plumbing, and many exterior projects require city review. We identify the permit path before demolition starts and verify contractor licensing through Utah DOPL.
  • 3. Change-order control: Hidden framing, rot, or utility conditions get documented with photos and written approval before they become surprise invoices. The FTC home-improvement scam guidance is the plain-English owner-protection reference.
  • 4. Roof integration: When an addition, deck, siding project, or remodel touches the roofline, we coordinate flashing, ventilation, ice-barrier, and drainage details as one system. IRC roof-ceiling rules such as R806.2 ventilation keep the roof assembly from being an afterthought.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Frame Restoration Utah handle more than roofing?

Yes. Beyond roofing we take on kitchen and bathroom remodels, home additions and build-outs, siding and deck work, and full-home renovations across the Wasatch Front. The same crews and project management that deliver our roofs run our general-contracting work, so you get one accountable team from start to finish.

Do you pull permits for remodels and additions in Utah?

Yes. We handle the permit process with your city's building department and schedule the required inspections. As a licensed Utah contractor (DOPL S280) we build to current code so your project passes inspection and protects your home's value.

Can you combine a roof replacement with a remodel or addition?

Often, yes — and it can save money. Bundling a reroof with an addition or exterior project means shared mobilization, fewer disruptions, and one coordinated schedule instead of juggling multiple contractors. We scope it together and sequence the work so the roof and the build line up.

What areas do you serve for general contracting?

We serve 40-plus communities across Utah's Wasatch Front — from Salt Lake City and the Salt Lake Valley to Utah County, Davis County, and the Heber Valley. If you're in our roofing service area, we can take on your remodel or build.
