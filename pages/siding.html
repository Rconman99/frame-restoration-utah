Utah is hard on exteriors. Freeze-thaw cycles open small cracks, high-elevation sun fades and embrittles vinyl, and spring and summer storms bring hail and gusty wind. Siding that is cracked, loose, or missing lets water reach the sheathing behind it, which is where rot and mold start.

Why coordinate siding replacement with the roof?

Coordinating siding with the roof matters because many leaks blamed on a Utah roof actually start where the wall meets it: missing kick-out flashing that dumps water behind siding, step flashing buried under old panels, or trim that was never sealed. Because Frame Restoration Utah is a roofing contractor first, it treats those transitions as one system. If you are replacing your roof, it is a good time to look at the siding next to it, and the other way around. See roof replacement and storm damage.

How do you check siding for hail or wind damage in Utah?

After a Utah hail or wind storm, check the sides of the house that faced the storm for cracked or holed vinyl, chipped fiber cement, dented trim, and panels that have come loose. Siding damage usually means the roof and gutters took hits too, so we inspect all three and document what we find. We document conditions with photos, moisture readings, and a written scope you can share with your insurer. If water already got behind the siding, our water damage and mold remediation teams can handle what is inside the wall.

Siding Replacement and Repair FAQs for Utah

What siding materials do you install?

We install and repair vinyl and fiber-cement siding, along with the exterior trim that goes with it. We match the style of your existing home where a full replacement is not needed.

Can hail and wind damage siding?

Yes. Hail can crack, chip, and dent siding, especially vinyl in cold weather, and strong wind can loosen or tear off panels. Siding damage often shows up alongside roof and gutter damage from the same storm, so we inspect them together.

Why coordinate siding with the roof?

Water often gets behind siding where the wall meets the roof: at step flashing, kick-out flashing, and around dormers. Coordinating the siding with the roof flashing details keeps those transitions watertight.

Is siding work available 24/7?

Siding repair and replacement is scheduled during business hours (Mon–Fri 8am–7pm, Sat 8am–6pm, Sun closed). If a storm has torn siding or roofing open, our 24/7 line covers emergency tarping and storm response, plus water and flood emergencies.

Can storm damage to siding be part of an insurance claim?

It depends on your policy and the storm. We document conditions with photos, moisture readings, and a written scope you can share with your insurer. Coverage decisions stay between you and your insurance company; we document and restore, we do not adjust claims.
