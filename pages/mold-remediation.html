Mold Remediation Steps in Salt Lake City

Frame Restoration Utah's mold remediation process in Salt Lake City and northern Utah has four steps: find the moisture source, fix it, contain the area and remove affected materials, then HEPA clean and dry.

Find the Moisture Source

Moisture readings and inspection of the affected area to find the leak, intrusion, or condensation feeding the growth.

Fix the Source

Plumbing, roof, ventilation, or drainage repairs so the area can stay dry. Roof and attic repairs are handled in-house.

Contain and Remove

The work area is contained to limit spread, and affected porous materials such as drywall and insulation are removed.

HEPA Clean and Dry

Remaining surfaces are HEPA vacuumed and cleaned, and the area is dried and checked before rebuild.

Where does mold grow in Utah homes?

In Utah homes, mold most often grows in attics, basements, behind walls and under cabinets, and around windows and exterior walls: anywhere water stayed longer than it should.

  • Attics: Roof leaks, ice dams, and bathroom fans vented into the attic. See roof repair.
  • Basements: After floods, snowmelt seepage, or a failed sump pump. See flood cleanup.
  • Behind walls and under cabinets: Slow plumbing and appliance leaks. See water damage restoration.
  • Around windows and exterior walls: Condensation in winter and water getting past siding or flashing. See siding replacement and repair.

Does spraying or painting over mold fix it?

No. Spraying a product over visible mold, or painting over it, does not remove the growth and does not stop it from returning in a Utah home. Neither does running a dehumidifier while a leak continues. Frame Restoration Utah would rather show you the source and fix it than sell a treatment that only covers the symptom.

We document conditions with photos, moisture readings, and a written scope you can share with your insurer. Coverage decisions stay between you and your insurance company; we document and restore, we do not adjust claims.

Mold Remediation FAQs for Salt Lake City and Utah

What causes mold in Utah homes if the climate is dry?

Mold needs moisture, and Utah homes get plenty of it from the inside: slow plumbing leaks, roof leaks and ice dams, flooded basements, poorly vented bathrooms and attics, and condensation in cold spots during winter. Dry outdoor air does not help a wall cavity that stays wet.

Do you test for mold?

We focus on finding the moisture source and the extent of the growth, using moisture readings and visual inspection of the affected areas. If you need independent laboratory testing, for example before a sale, an independent tester can provide it.

Why fix the moisture source first?

Because mold comes back if the material stays wet. Removing growth without stopping the leak, condensation, or water intrusion only resets the clock. That is why every mold job starts with finding and fixing the source.

Is mold remediation available 24/7?

No. Mold remediation is scheduled during business hours (Mon–Fri 8am–7pm, Sat 8am–6pm, Sun closed). If active water is causing the problem, call our 24/7 water emergency line at 435-292-8802.

Can you fix a roof leak that caused attic mold?

Yes. Frame Restoration Utah is a licensed roofing contractor, so we can repair the roof leak or ventilation problem behind attic mold as well as remediate the affected materials.
