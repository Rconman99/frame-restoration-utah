How the calculator sizes your roof

A sloped roof has more surface than the ground under it. The calculator adds about 10 percent to your footprint for eaves and rake overhangs, then multiplies by a pitch factor: about 1.05 for a 4/12 slope, 1.12 for 6/12, 1.20 for 8/12 and 1.30 for 10/12. A 2,000 sq ft footprint with an 8/12 pitch comes out near 26 squares.

If a past quote or insurance report lists your roof in squares, switch to "roof squares" and enter that number instead. It will be closer than any footprint estimate.

What a calculator can't see

Two Utah roofs of the same size can price very differently. Decking condition, the number of layers to tear off, ventilation, flashing, chimneys and skylights, access, elevation and the exact product you choose all move the number. Roof Cost Calculator FAQs

How do I calculate how many squares my roof is?

Measure the ground footprint of the house, including an attached garage, add about 10 percent for eaves and rake overhangs, then multiply by the pitch factor: about 1.05 for a 4/12 slope, 1.12 for 6/12, 1.20 for 8/12 and 1.30 for 10/12. Divide by 100. One roofing square is 100 square feet of roof surface.

Why does pitch matter so much on Utah roofs?

A steeper roof has more surface than the footprint under it, and it takes more safety setup and labor to work. Many Wasatch Back and mountain homes are steeper than a typical valley rambler, so the same footprint can mean a much bigger roof.

Is an online roof cost calculator accurate?

It is a planning tool. It cannot see decking condition, how many layers need to come off, flashing, ventilation, access, elevation or the exact product you pick. Frame Restoration Utah prices every roof from a free inspection and a written scope.

What should a written roof replacement quote include?

Full tear-off or overlay, underlayment type, ice and water shield in valleys and at eaves, drip edge, flashing replacement, ridge vent, pipe boots, the shingle product by name, and cleanup and haul-away. If a bid just says replace roof with one number, ask for the detail.
